Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Terms of Service

Défilé is a runway walk coaching app. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the controls you have. We keep it short because the app collects very little.

Who is responsible

Défilé is operated by Vlad Purza, Romania, who is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: support.defile@gmail.com.

Your videos stay on your device

Recordings and clips you choose from your photo library are analysed on your device using Google ML Kit. The video itself is never uploaded and never kept: the app deletes its copy of the clip as soon as the analysis finishes (your original stays in your photo library). The camera records without sound. No photos, video frames or audio ever leave your phone.

What is sent when you analyse a walk

To generate coaching, the app sends our backend a small set of numbers derived from your clip:

  • your scores and measurements (for example shoulder level, stride length, arm swing);
  • body-position points: the positions of 17 joints (such as shoulders, hips, knees and ankles) on about eight frames of the clip, plus a summary of how each joint moved;
  • basic clip details: camera angle, length, frame rate, resolution, and whether it was recorded or picked from your library;
  • how this walk compares with your recent walks (score trends), if you have any.

Joint positions describe posture and movement only. We do not use them, or any other data, to identify you biometrically.

What we store

  • Your account email address, and the random account ID that each of your records is stored under.
  • Your profile: your name, your experience and skill level, any goals or focus areas you set, and one shoulder-width ratio averaged from your head-on walks, used to recognise the camera angle of later clips.
  • Each session's scores, measurements and written coaching, together with the camera angle and whether the clip was recorded or imported. The body-position points are not stored.
  • A daily count of analyses, used only to apply a fair-use limit.

That is the full extent of it. Every record is private to your account and protected by row-level security in our database. Some settings (such as units and reminder time) are saved only on your device.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide the service you asked for (your account, analysis, coaching and history): performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the service fair and secure (the daily analysis limit and abuse prevention): our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. The AI-written coaching is informational only.

Who can see it

Only you. The database enforces that you can read and write your own data and that no other user can reach it.

Service providers

We use Supabase to host your account and session data, and Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In for authentication. Account emails (confirmation and password reset) are sent through Google’s email service. Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your device and involve no server. These providers process your data only to deliver these services. We do not use any advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How coaching is generated

To turn your walk into written coaching, our backend forwards the data described in “What is sent when you analyse a walk” to OpenAI, the provider of the AI model (GPT) that writes your feedback. Only these numbers are sent — never your video, photos, name or email. OpenAI processes this data solely to return the coaching text and, under its API terms, does not use it to train its models. OpenAI keeps a copy of what is sent for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, after which it is deleted, unless a longer period is required by law.

International transfers

OpenAI processes data on servers in the United States, and our other providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, the transfer is covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses in that provider’s data processing agreement, or by an adequacy decision such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address; Apple then provides us a private relay address. We treat it the same as any other account email.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, profile and sessions until you delete them. The daily analysis count is overwritten each day. When you delete your account, everything is removed from our live database immediately; residual copies in our hosting provider’s routine backups are overwritten within about 7 days.

Your controls and rights

You can sign out at any time. To erase your account and every piece of data we hold — permanently — use Delete account in Profile in the app. This deletes your authentication record, profile, and all session data immediately.

Under the GDPR you also have the right to access your data, have it corrected, erased or exported (portability), to restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email us and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Romania this is the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP).

Our website

This section covers our website, defile.app, including its guides.

  • No cookies, no ads, no tracking pixels. The website sets no cookies and contains no advertising, social-media or marketing trackers. Fonts and images are served from our own site.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type only to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse.
  • Visit statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views in aggregate (for example, how many people read a guide and which site referred them). It uses no cookies, does not store your IP address and does not follow you across other websites.
  • Links to other services. Download buttons open the Apple App Store (and Google Play, once available); the “Save to Pinterest” link opens Pinterest. Nothing is sent to these services until you click, and their own privacy policies apply once you do. App Store links carry a campaign tag naming the guide you came from, so we can see which guides lead to downloads in aggregate, never who you are.
  • Legal basis. Our legitimate interest in running a secure website and understanding which guides are useful (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Images. Images of people on the website and in our guides are AI-generated illustrations.

Children

Défilé is not directed to children. You must be at least 16 years old to use it, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
