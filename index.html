Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · Terms of Service

Défilé is a runway walk coaching app. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the controls you have. We keep it short because the app collects very little.

Your videos stay on your device

Recordings and clips you choose from your photo library are analysed on your device using Google ML Kit. The video itself is never uploaded and never kept: the app deletes its copy of the clip as soon as the analysis finishes (your original stays in your photo library). Only the resulting pose measurements and scores are sent to our backend so we can generate coaching feedback.

What we store

  • Your account email address.
  • Your profile: your name and any goals you set.
  • Each session's scores, metrics and written coaching (numbers derived from your walk).
  • A daily count of analyses, used only to apply a fair-use limit.

That is the full extent of it. Every record is private to your account and protected by row-level security in our database.

Who can see it

Only you. The database enforces that you can read and write your own data and that no other user can reach it.

Service providers

We use Supabase to host your account and session data, and Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In for authentication. Account emails (confirmation and password reset) are sent through Google’s email service. Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your device and involve no server. These providers process your data only to deliver these services. We do not use any advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How coaching is generated

To turn your walk into written coaching, we send the numeric measurements and scores described above to OpenAI, the provider of the AI model (GPT) that writes your feedback. Only these numbers are sent — never your video, photos, or name. OpenAI processes this data solely to return the coaching text and, under its API terms, does not use it to train its models. OpenAI keeps a copy of what is sent for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, after which it is deleted, unless a longer period is required by law.

Where this happens. OpenAI processes these measurements on servers in the United States, so this step transfers your data outside the European Economic Area. The transfer is covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses in OpenAI’s data processing addendum. As above, what leaves your device is a short list of numbers — never your video, photos, or name.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address; Apple then provides us a private relay address. We treat it the same as any other account email.

Your controls

You can sign out at any time. To erase your account and every piece of data we hold — permanently — use Delete account in Profile in the app. This deletes your authentication record, profile, and all session data immediately.

Children

Défilé is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
